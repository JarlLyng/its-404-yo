How it works FAQ

Support

Something not working, a pack that won’t convert, or an idea for the app? I’d like to hear it.

Email

Write to support@iamjarl.com. In the app, Help › Send Feedback… opens the same address with your app and macOS version already filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send.

If a file won’t convert or the SP-404 still says “Unsupported File”, the most useful thing to include is what the app showed for that file: the format line under its name, and any reason or warning next to it.

Where SP-404 people talk

There’s a thread for the app on Elektronauts, and I read it: It’s 404, yo! on Elektronauts. Questions asked there help the next person with the same pack too.

Bug reports and code

The app is open source. If you’re comfortable on GitHub, you can also open an issue.
